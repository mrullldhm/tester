1. **bug — `strategies/rlm.py:227,281` — TF provenance overwrites surviving points.** A registry entry stores one weight per TF, but multiple point IDs from that TF can reference it. Withdrawing either deletes that TF’s entire contribution. Reproduction: one HTF point plus two nested lower-TF points; withdrawing the first lower point and HTF point leaves the second PID registered but no live level. **Fix:** store contributions by full PID; derive the touch’s maximum weight from surviving PIDs.

2. **bug — `strategies/rlm.py:164,181,284` — stale strength shares survive level death.** `_kill()` removes ownership but retains `base`; withdrawing an ownerless point leaves its registry entry behind. Re-registering that extreme can reuse its old base. Probe: level strength was 2 while recorded shares totaled 3; withdrawing one touch left another touch with zero strength. **Fix:** delete bases on death/full withdrawal, clean empty registry entries, and initialize a resurrected touch’s base from zero.

3. **bug — `strategies/rlm.py:498` — improved fills distort the break-even threshold.** `(T-E)//3` reconstructs risk using the actual fill and a target calculated from the intended limit. Example: limit 1000, stop 990, target 1030, fill 995 gives “R” 11, versus intended risk 10 and actual risk 5. **Fix:** preserve the signal’s frozen TP1 (`limit + intended risk`) and move the stop to actual entry after that threshold; explicitly document any alternative convention.

4. **bug — `strategies/rlm.py:354,360,386` — mixed-contract rejection is ineffective.** `c_start` selects the contract containing `cd.first`, making `cd.first < c_start` impossible. ATR grouping by the last bar can also include mixed-contract OHLC. The bounded sample contained six mixed 4h and six mixed daily candles; none were rejected by this comparison. No mixed signal-TF candles occurred in that sample. **Fix:** explicitly require matching contracts at `first`, `last`, and signal availability; exclude mixed candles before ATR seeding and validate source extreme/reference candle endpoints.

5. **bug — `studies/rlm/grid.py:58,84`; `studies/rlm/screen.py:29` — screen integrity is unchecked.** Resume trusts filenames without a code/data fingerprint. Merge accepts incomplete families; missing neighbours disappear from the denominator. Consequently, stale or partial results can appear comparable and pass screening. **Fix:** fingerprint strategy/engine/data/configuration, reject incompatible parts, and require exactly 13,824 unique expected combinations before screening.
